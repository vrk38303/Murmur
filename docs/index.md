# Murmur

A private on-device voice-note recorder that transcribes your conversations and weaves their topics into a personal mind map. Everything stays on your phone.

## What is Murmur?

Murmur is a voice-note app for people who actually listen back to their conversations. Tap once to record. Murmur transcribes you in real time using Apple's on-device speech engine. When you stop, it summarises what was said in two or three sentences, lifts any action items into a checklist, and connects the topics across every conversation you've recorded into a mind map you can search.

Nothing leaves your phone. There is no account to sign up for. There is no cloud to opt out of. Audio is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, keyed to your device. Transcription runs on-device using Apple SpeechAnalyzer or SFSpeechRecognizer. Summarisation runs on-device using Apple Intelligence on supported hardware, or a bundled fallback engine.

Murmur is rated 4+ and works on any iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Apple Intelligence summaries require iPhone 15 Pro or newer.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Murmur record phone calls?**
No. iOS does not allow any app to access cellular phone-call audio. Murmur records only the device microphone, which means anything you speak aloud or play through speakerphone. You must explicitly tap the red record button to begin every recording.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone, encrypted at rest. There is no Murmur server.

**How do I export my data?**
Settings → Privacy → Export library. Murmur produces a `.zip` containing every transcript, summary, and topic graph (audio optional). Send it to Files, AirDrop, or anywhere else.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → Privacy → Delete all data. Confirm with Face ID. Every recording, transcript, summary, topic, and the encryption key are wiped. There is no recovery.

**Why am I being reminded to ask for consent?**
Recording someone without their knowledge is illegal in many places and unkind everywhere. Murmur shows a one-time education sheet on first use and a per-call reminder banner.
